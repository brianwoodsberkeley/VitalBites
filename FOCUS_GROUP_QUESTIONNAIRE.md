# VitalBites User Focus Group Questionnaire

## Session Setup

**Product**: VitalBites — an AI-powered recipe recommendation system that personalizes meal suggestions based on a user's health conditions and body metrics.

**Session length**: 60–90 minutes

**Format**: Guided walkthrough of the app followed by structured discussion. Participants should have completed registration, viewed at least 3 recipes, and marked at least 1 as cooked or skipped before the discussion begins.

---

## Section 1 — First Impressions

1. What was your initial reaction when you first saw the landing page? Did it clearly communicate what the app does?
2. Did anything on the landing page make you hesitate before signing up?
3. How would you describe VitalBites to a friend in one sentence?

---

## Section 2 — Registration & Onboarding

1. The registration asks you to select your health conditions from a list of 20 (e.g., Hypertension, Diabetes, Weight Management, Anemia). Was the list easy to understand? Were there any conditions you expected to see but didn't?
2. After registering, you were asked for optional body metrics (height, weight, age, sex, activity level). How did you feel about being asked for this information? Did you fill it in or skip it?
3. If you skipped the body metrics step, what would have made you more likely to fill it in?
4. After submitting your profile, a loading screen appeared saying "Retrieving your recipe recommendations." Did the wait feel appropriate, too long, or too short?

---

## Section 3 — Dashboard & Recommendations

1. When you first saw your recommended recipes, did they feel relevant to your health conditions? Can you give a specific example?
2. Were there any recipes that surprised you — either positively or negatively?
3. The dashboard shows three tabs: Recommendations, Cooked, and Skipped. Was this organization intuitive? Is there a different way you'd prefer to browse recipes?
4. How many recipes did you look at before finding one you'd actually want to cook? What does that tell you?
5. Did you use the "Get New Recipes" button? If so, how did the new batch compare to the first?

---

## Section 4 — Recipe Detail Page

1. When viewing a recipe, you see ingredients with their micro-nutrients listed (e.g., Spinach → iron, folate, vitamin K1). How useful was this information? Did it influence your decision to cook or skip?
2. The "Helps With" section shows which of your health conditions the recipe addresses. Did you notice this? Did it build confidence in the recommendation?
3. Were the cooking instructions clear and complete enough to actually prepare the meal?
4. Did you notice the YouTube video link for the recipe? Would you use it?
5. The "Buy Recipe Now" section shows placeholders for grocery delivery services (Amazon Fresh, Instacart, etc.). How important would this integration be to you?

---

## Section 5 — Cook & Skip Actions

1. When you marked a recipe as "Cooked," what motivated that action? Were you planning to cook it, or had you already made it?
2. When you skipped a recipe, what was the reason? (e.g., didn't like the ingredients, too complex, not relevant to your conditions, dietary preference not captured)
3. Did you use the "Undo" feature on the Cooked or Skipped tabs? Was it easy to find?
4. Would you want a "Save for Later" option separate from Cook and Skip?

---

## Section 6 — Profile Editing

1. Did you go back and edit your health profile after the initial setup? If so, what prompted you to do so?
2. Was it clear how to find the profile editing page?
3. If you changed your health conditions, did you notice a difference in the recipes recommended afterward?

---

## Section 7 — AI & Personalization Transparency

1. VitalBites uses AI to recommend recipes in three ways:
  - **Ingredient filtering**: Automatically removes recipes containing ingredients that are clinically inappropriate for your conditions (e.g., high-sodium foods for hypertension)
    - **Nutrient target calculation**: Computes your personalized daily targets for calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sodium, and more — based on validated medical formulas and dietary guidelines
    - **Nutrient-distance ranking**: Ranks recipes by how well their ingredients align with your personal nutrient targets
    Were you aware the app was doing this? Does knowing this change how you feel about the recommendations?
2. The nutrient targets are calculated using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation (a peer-reviewed metabolic formula) and adjusted based on clinical guidelines from organizations like the AHA, ADA, and KDIGO. Does citing specific medical sources increase your trust in the recommendations?
3. Would you want to see your computed nutrient targets displayed somewhere in the app (e.g., "Your daily targets: 2,066 kcal, 98g protein, 1,500mg sodium")? Where?
4. Would you want to understand why a specific recipe was recommended or filtered out? For example: "This recipe was excluded because it contains soy sauce (high sodium), which conflicts with your Hypertension profile."
5. How comfortable are you with the app using your health conditions and body metrics to personalize recipes? Is there any data you'd prefer not to share?

---

## Section 8 — Trust & Safety

1. Would you trust this app's recommendations enough to follow them without consulting a doctor or dietitian?
2. The app states that nutrient targets are based on published dietary guidelines — not medical advice. Is this disclaimer important to you? Where would you expect to see it?
3. If the app recommended a recipe that you knew was wrong for your condition, how would that affect your trust? What would you want to happen next?
4. How would you feel about sharing your VitalBites profile link with your doctor or nutritionist?

---

## Section 9 — Sharing & Social

1. Did you notice the "Share" button on recipe pages? Did you use it?
2. The app generates a public link for your dashboard and individual recipes that anyone can view (without seeing your health conditions). How do you feel about this?
3. Would you share a recipe from VitalBites on social media or with friends/family? What would make you more likely to do so?

---

## Section 10 — Overall Experience & Improvements

1. On a scale of 1–10, how likely are you to continue using VitalBites? Why?
2. What was the single most valuable thing the app did for you?
3. What was the most frustrating part of the experience?
4. What one feature would you add to VitalBites that doesn't exist today?
5. Would you pay for a premium version of this app? If so, what features would need to be included?
6. Is there anything else you'd like to share about your experience?

---

## Demographic Information (Optional)

- Age range: 18–24 / 25–34 / 35–44 / 45–54 / 55–64 / 65+
- How many health conditions did you select during registration?
- Do you currently follow a specific diet (e.g., low-sodium, diabetic, keto, vegetarian)?
- How often do you cook at home? (Daily / Several times a week / Once a week / Rarely)
- Have you used other recipe or meal planning apps? If so, which ones?
